How it works

1

Define your plan

Write it in a simple YAML format — or describe it in plain English and let the engine draft it.

2

Drop in your deals

CSV, Excel, or a warehouse export (Snowflake, BigQuery, DuckDB). Messy column names are matched automatically.

3

Get explained payouts

Per-rep commission statements plus an immutable audit trail for every single number.
